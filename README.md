# SEP10 Freedom Project
by **Kaitlyn Dong**

## Context
I am a student at [HSTAT](https://www.hstat.org/) in the [Software Engineering Program](https://hstatsep.github.io/). The "Freedom Project" for SEP10 is a year-long project all about making a website that informs the viewer of the current and future innovations in the topic of my choosing.

For my project, I chose the topic of **Buisness**. The purpose of the freedom project is to show people my ideas on future technologies that could help business and show using existing technologies that are using in business. Anothr purpose of the freedom project is using everything that I learned in SEP to create a website on my topic to show everyone.

I used my knowledge of HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, and Github to make a website on buisness in technology. I chosed to independently study **Aframe** in order to help me make my website and show a model of my future innvoation ideas like the model of the infinite storage usb.

## Implications
##### PROS
* Give inventors ideas for technologies that they could create in the future
* Encourage people working in business to use helpful existing technologies that can make their jobs easier and more efficent
* Help people learn about existing technologies and how they impact business
##### CONS
* The infinite usb storage future innovation idea may be impossible and extremely difficult to create with current technology
* Business may be heavily dependent on technology which could lead to possible problems like system failue.
